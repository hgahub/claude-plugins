# The high class — its extra rules in steps 4, 5 and 7

Read from `SKILL.md` when the class is `high`: provisional from step 0's label, or from `HGA risk`. The "Risk
class" table there lists every high gate; these are the sentences that steps 4, 5 and 7 add. Step 6's high rule is
in `live-test.md`, step 8's in its table.

- **Step 4:** With a `high` class the maintainer decides (see "Risk class").
- **Step 5:** **high:** every new test needs its base failure; no new test means the step is not done.
- **Step 7,** the PR's **Risk** section: for **high** also **Rollback**: how to undo it after the merge, and what a revert alone
  does not undo
